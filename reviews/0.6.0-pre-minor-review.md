## Fix before the tag

- [ ] **M1 (major, verified by 2 lenses: correctness + docs). The CHANGELOG "Verdict changes" section is incomplete and partly false** (`CHANGELOG.md` ~L51-60). Measured against the 0.5.2 tarball with a 10x10 / 18x18 undersized button:
  - (a) `within.width` or `within.height` = Infinity drew in 0.5.2 (viewBox `-8 -8 Infinity 430`) with the undersized legend entry. HEAD gives legend `[]` plus the unusable-within note. The section does not name it.
  - (b) An overflowing extent drops every legend entry, undersized included, and the section does not mention it. Two inputs cause this: `pad: 1e308`, and a records-only input with no option involved (boxes at x=-1e308/w=1.5e308 and x=1.7e308). The second is a **record shape** whose verdict changed. Neither stability grid can reach it.
  - (c) So "No `undersized` answer changes for any option value" is false, because `pad` and `within` are options.
  - (d) "A `within` with a null or missing field drew in 0.5.x (coerced to 0)" is false for missing/undefined fields (they gave NaN and drew nothing) and for null width/height. Only null x/y drew.
  - **Fix:** rewrite the section from the measured table:
    1. A `within` with null x or y, or an Infinity width or height, drew in 0.5.x and now draws nothing, so its records lose their legend entries, undersized included.
    2. An extent that overflows, whether from `pad` of about 1e308 or more or from record bounds near `Number.MAX_VALUE`, now returns no legend, undersized included.
    3. Narrow the sentence to: no undersized change for `maxCaption`/`fontSize`/`minLabelHeight`.
  - Also align the Changed bullet at L33 ("coerced them to 0 or NaN") so it agrees.
  - **Check that clears it:** a driven scenario. Run the five inputs (within width=Infinity, within height=Infinity, within x=null, `pad: 1e308`, the records-only overflow pair) through the published 0.5.2 dist and HEAD, compare the legends, and confirm each difference is named in the section and nothing in the section is contradicted.
